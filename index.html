About Us

A Little Shop with a Big Heart

Jerose HandCraft began as a small passion project in Digos City — turning yarn, beads and fabric into meaningful keepsakes. Today, we continue to craft each piece by hand, with patience, love and attention to every tiny detail.

100% handmade in the Philippines
Sustainable, locally-sourced materials
Custom orders & personalization
Fast & careful delivery

Our Mission

To bring warmth and joy through thoughtfully handcrafted goods that celebrate Filipino artistry.

Our Vision

To be Digos City's most loved handmade brand — known for quality, creativity, and heart.
